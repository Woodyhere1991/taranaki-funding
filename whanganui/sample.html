Tip of the week

Check the funder's area and eligibility rules before writing your application. For gaming trusts, check their current venue list. Keep your latest financial statements, bank account proof and quotes ready in one folder.
Know a club treasurer or secretary? Forward this to them, or share the signup link: https://nzgrants.org/whanganui/
