Frequently asked questions

Free weekly email for clubs, schools, marae and community groups in Taranaki, Whanganui and Manawatū.

Is the email free?

Yes. It is free.

When does the email arrive?

You get one email each Monday morning.

Which areas does it cover?

There is a separate edition for Taranaki, Whanganui and Manawatū. Taranaki covers New Plymouth, Stratford and South Taranaki. Whanganui covers the Whanganui District. Manawatū covers Palmerston North City and the Manawatū District.

How do I confirm my email?

Check your inbox for a confirmation email. Click the link in it to start getting the Monday email. If you can't see it, check your spam or promotions folder. Once you have confirmed your subscription, you can receive the Monday funding email. Your first email will arrive next Monday morning. If no confirmation arrives, email nzfundinglist@gmail.com for help.

How do I unsubscribe?

You can unsubscribe any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email or replying UNSUBSCRIBE.

What happens to my email address?

We only collect your email address, and only to send you this newsletter. We never sell or share it. Your email address is stored with our email provider, Brevo, only to send this newsletter.

Is this linked to a funder?

This is a free guide that isn't linked to any funder, so always confirm dates on the funder's website.

Can I see or delete my details?

You can ask to see or delete your details by emailing nzfundinglist@gmail.com.

Who do I contact?

Contact nzfundinglist@gmail.com.
