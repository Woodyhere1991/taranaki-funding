Your first email arrives on Monday.

What you get

  • Deadlines from Lotteries, the gaming trusts and the councils, all in one place
  • A heads-up before big rounds close
  • One practical tip a week to make applying easier
  • Links straight to each funder

Privacy

We only collect your email address, and only to send you this newsletter. We never sell or share it. You can unsubscribe any time by replying UNSUBSCRIBE to any email, and you can ask to see or delete your details by emailing nzfundinglist@gmail.com. This is a free guide that isn't linked to any funder, so always confirm dates on the funder's website.
