Editorial independence and advertising policy

How we choose grants

We list grants that are open to community groups in our regions. Each deadline is checked against the funder's own website, and we link to the funder's page so you can check it yourself. We never make up grants, dates or amounts.

Ads never affect listings

Advertisers cannot pay to add, remove, move or reword any grant listing. Grant listings and ads are kept separate.

How ads appear

  • At most one ad per issue.
  • Each ad is labelled "Advertisement" at the top, so it is clear it is not our own content.
  • No ads in welcome emails, signup confirmations or invites.
  • The newsletters stay free.

Supporters and funders

Councils, community foundations, companies and other funders, including funders whose grants we list, may back or sponsor an edition. When they do:

  • The support is clearly disclosed with a "Supported by" credit in every issue it appears in.
  • A "Supported by" credit is just a name, logo and link. Anything more promotional is labelled "Advertisement".
  • A supporter's own grants are listed by the same rules as everyone else's. No extra, earlier or better placement, and no removal of rivals' grants.
  • Supporters never see or approve content before it is sent.
  • Support can never affect which grants we list or how we describe them.

Errors and corrections

If you spot a wrong date, amount or link, reply to any issue or email nzfundinglist@gmail.com. We check it against the funder's website and correct it in the next issue.

Unsubscribing

Every issue has an unsubscribe link, or you can reply UNSUBSCRIBE. Link unsubscribes take effect straight away, and we action replies within 5 working days, usually the same day.
